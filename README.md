i study computer science, business, and ppe at unc, and build things at the seam between people and the systems that try to understand them.

mostly i'm curious about **how people come to know things** — how trust shapes belief, and what it would take for software to build a useful model of the person in front of it. that pulls me toward digital personas, proactive intelligence, and education: systems that notice what you need to learn next, without sanding away the judgment, curiosity, and struggle that make learning worth doing.
